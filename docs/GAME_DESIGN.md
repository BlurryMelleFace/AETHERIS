# AETHERIS — Game Design Vision

## High concept
An atmospheric, procedurally generated, fully editable 2D survival sandbox RPG. Combine Terraria-style mining, equipment progression and bosses with Valheim-style lighting, exploration and shelters; Minecraft-style creativity; Subnautica-like ocean mystery; and Starbound-like interplanetary journeys.

## Design pillars
1. Discovery first: exploration is its own reward; avoid mandatory quest chains.
2. Living worlds: forests, weather, caves, oceans and creatures feel interconnected.
3. Meaningful progression: bosses and discoveries unlock new capabilities.
4. Creative ownership: flexible structures and outposts are useful, not merely decorative.
5. Cozy and terrifying: a warm lakeside home contrasts with abyssal expeditions.

## World structure
Surface forests / islands / mountains, subterranean caves and ruins, oceans / trenches, underworld, sky islands, and eventually multiple planets. Each planetary world is independently seeded and stored. Horizontal planet wraparound is an optional later design decision.

## Art direction
Side-on 2D, high-detail pixel art with restrained bloom, shaders, parallax layers, dynamic shadows, weather, and clear gameplay silhouettes. Concept art is mood reference, not an in-game asset pack.

## First milestone
One forest, a mineable ground, one cave, a simple shelter, and an exploratory loop. Do not scope oceans, spacecraft, or multiplayer into this milestone.

## Long-term
Underwater bases, boats and submarines, boss-gated progression, star systems, spacecraft, planets and eventually multiplayer.
